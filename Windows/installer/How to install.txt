BibleTime screen saver for Windows
==================================

BibleTime tells the time through scripture: the chapter is the hour and the
verse is the minute. At 3:16 you see John 3:16, and a new verse appears every
minute.

This is the "portable" version. It does not need administrator rights, which
makes it handy on a work or school computer. (If you can, the regular
installer, BibleTime-Windows-Setup.exe, is even simpler.)


How to install
--------------

1. Unzip this folder first, if you have not already: right-click the zip file,
   choose "Extract All...", then click "Extract".

2. Move BibleTime.scr somewhere it can stay, for example your Documents
   folder. Windows runs it from wherever it is, so do not delete it later.

3. Right-click BibleTime.scr and choose "Install".
   (On Windows 11 you may need to click "Show more options" first.)

4. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then
   "Run anyway". This appears because BibleTime is a free project and is not
   signed with a paid certificate. It is safe to continue.
   If it says "Smart App Control blocked an app that may be unsafe" instead,
   there is no way to allow it on that PC: Smart App Control only runs
   signed apps.

5. The Screen Saver Settings window opens with BibleTime selected.
   Click "Settings..." to choose the look, the translation and more,
   then click "OK".


How to change the options later
-------------------------------

Open Settings > Personalization > Lock screen > Screen saver, make sure
BibleTime is selected, and click "Settings...".


How to remove it
----------------

1. Open the Screen Saver Settings window (see above) and choose another
   screen saver, or "(None)". Click "OK".
2. Delete BibleTime.scr.
