Unicode Character Database
--------------------------

The Windows version (BibleTime.scr) contains tables generated from the Unicode
Character Database 15.1.0 (UnicodeData.txt, SpecialCasing.txt,
DerivedCoreProperties.txt, extracted/DerivedNumericType.txt,
auxiliary/GraphemeBreakProperty.txt, auxiliary/GraphemeBreakTest.txt and
emoji/emoji-data.txt), used to count characters and compare names the way the
Mac version does. They live in Windows/src/core/GraphemeTables.inc and
Windows/tests/GraphemeBreakTest.inc and are generated by
Windows/tools/gen_unicode_tables.py.
